She had met CHEN-YI once before. He had been in debt to the organization, and she witnessed him being beaten by members of ARANAMI for failing to repay on time.
While the members were away, AFURE happened to come across CHEN-YI lying on the ground. Despite everything, he looked up at her and smiled.
She found it absurd that someone could still smile after taking that kind of beating — but it sparked her curiosity. AFURE approached him and spoke to him.
