AFURE told CHEN-YI that she would support him financially and urged him to go to university.

CHEN-YI was deeply flustered at first. He said he couldn't accept such an offer when he still hadn't fully repaid his debt to her father.

AFURE pushed back hard. She told him that if she said to do it, he would do it — and not to worry about her father. She was afraid of her father's anger herself, but felt that for CHEN-YI's sake, she could endure it. Somewhere along the way, AFURE had fallen for him.

CHEN-YI accepted the offer, somewhat against his will. But he couldn't quite hide how excited he was.

CHEN-YI cut back on his work hours and began preparing for the university entrance exam. Having loved reading as a child, he applied to a creative writing program and was successfully admitted. It wasn't a prestigious university, but it was close to home and easy to commute to — and more than anything, he was simply overjoyed to have the chance to study at all.

AFURE said she wanted to visit CHEN-YI's home under the pretext of looking after his siblings. CHEN-YI objected, saying his place was too humble, but AFURE pushed back hard once again.

CHEN-YI had no way to refuse her. AFURE visited his home for the very first time.
