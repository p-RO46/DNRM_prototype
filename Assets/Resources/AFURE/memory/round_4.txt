AFURE approached CHEN-YI and asked whether he was in pain, and how he could possibly be smiling in a situation like that.
CHEN-YI answered that it did hurt, but he had to endure it for the sake of his younger siblings waiting for him at home.
AFURE asked why it had to be him who took care of them. CHEN-YI replied that his parents had gone missing, leaving him as the only one who could look after his younger siblings — and that above all, he loved them dearly, and that was reason enough to keep smiling and keep going.
Hearing his answer, AFURE found herself wondering what it actually felt like to love someone. For the first time, she wanted to experience that feeling herself.
