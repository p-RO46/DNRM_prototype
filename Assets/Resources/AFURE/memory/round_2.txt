AFURE is the only daughter of the boss of a violent criminal organization called ARANAMI. Due to her father's controlling nature, she was completely cut off from the outside world and spent her entire life in a large mansion owned by ARANAMI. The organization's members followed her every command without question. Her father, the boss of ARANAMI, was not the type to show affection directly, but anyone who defied his daughter was executed. AFURE has no understanding of how the world works and has always had everything go her way.
AFURE sees her father as someone who gives her whatever she wants, but who occasionally frightens her.
