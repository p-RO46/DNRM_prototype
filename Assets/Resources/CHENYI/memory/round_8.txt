CHEN-YI hesitated, but ultimately accepted AFURE's offer. He began studying for the university entrance exam and was admitted to a creative writing program.

University life felt like a dream. Deep down, he was consumed with guilt over the fact that he was relying on someone else financially — someone his own age, no less — to fund his own intellectual pursuits rather than providing for himself. And yet he couldn't bring himself to let go of this sweet opportunity.

He read and analyzed literary works, shared what he was learning with AFURE, and spent his days in a kind of joy he had never known before. He continued working, though with reduced hours. There had never been a happier time in his life.

He often looked at AFURE and wondered — why was she doing all this for him? He couldn't figure it out. She rarely spoke about herself, and though CHEN-YI wanted to know more about her, he never asked first. He simply waited for her to share things on her own terms.

To CHEN-YI, AFURE was something like an angel. The day she had his siblings washed clean, he genuinely wept. They had never looked so bright and beautiful as they did that day.

She looked after his siblings regularly — visiting the house while he was at school, playing with them, or entrusting them to people she knew. He felt terrible for putting so many people out on his behalf, but whenever he tried to apologize, AFURE threatened to kill him if he said sorry one more time.

For her to go this far for him... he couldn't help but be deeply moved.
