CHEN-YI recalled that he had gone to university with the help of a benefactor, though he couldn't remember who that person was.

He had studied creative writing, and had been endlessly happy there — finally able to read and write literature to his heart's content.

But what had become of his young siblings during that time? Who had been looking after them? He couldn't remember how old he had been when he attended, nor who his benefactor was — but what he could remember, without any doubt, was how happy that period of his life had been.
