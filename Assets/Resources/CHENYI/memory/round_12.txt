CHEN-YI had been growing increasingly uncomfortable around AFURE, though the discomfort was entirely one-sided. As his life had grown more settled, the memories of selling his siblings had been slowly returning.

He had come to feel that her kindness was in fact a consequence of his own sin — the sin of selling his siblings away. It was unbearable. He found it harder and harder to even look at her face. That was ultimately what led to their fight.

There were days when the guilt made him want to die. But because his siblings' disappearance was part of what had kept him alive until now, he couldn't bring himself to throw that life away carelessly.

Unable to bear the guilt, instead of turning the gun on himself, he turned it elsewhere. Toward AFURE. Her father, her organization ARANAMI — they were the ones who had taken his siblings away.

If they had never made that proposal in the first place, CHEN-YI would never have had to live in this kind of agony. The three of them could have simply met their end together. There had been no need to drag himself through life all this time.

He hypnotized himself into believing that all the happiness AFURE had shown him was in fact compensation extracted through the sacrifice of his siblings. And so she began to feel like the root cause of everything. If he could just remove her, it seemed like everything would return to how it should have been.

CHEN-YI lured her to his home. He tore apart the stuffed animals — the false siblings that carried memories of AFURE — because he could no longer stand to look at them. The moment AFURE opened the front door, CHEN-YI drove a kitchen knife into her abdomen.

"It's all your fault," he murmured. Immediately after, something struck him and he lost consciousness.

When he opened his eyes, he was here.
