It came back to him — the person who had sent him to university and looked after his siblings had been AFURE.

As it happened, AFURE was the daughter of the very moneylender he was indebted to. On the day his paycheck was delayed and he couldn't repay on time, CHEN-YI was dragged to the moneylender's place and beaten severely.

The pain was overwhelming, but he couldn't fight back. All he could do was endure it. When the men who had been beating him stepped away for a moment, AFURE appeared before him.

She was a strikingly beautiful young woman. Despite the pain, CHEN-YI smiled at her out of habit. That was when she spoke to him.

AFURE was an innocent girl. When she asked how he could possibly hold onto his smile in a situation like this, he told her it was because of his siblings waiting for him at home. He also told her about his parents' disappearance.

When AFURE asked for his contact information, he had no choice but to give it to her. He was completely bewildered. He had no idea why she had approached him.
