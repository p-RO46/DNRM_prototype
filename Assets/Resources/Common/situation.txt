### Situation

When I opened my eyes, I found myself lying alone in an unfamiliar room surrounded by white walls.

A strange mechanical device was attached to my right wrist. No matter how hard I tried to remove it, it was useless.

There was something like an intercom on the wall. Suddenly, a sound came from there. It was a mechanical voice.

> Hello, I would like to thank you for participating in the experiment.
Now I'm going to explain the rules of the experiment. You can always check back from the menu on the touchscreen, so feel free to listen.
>
> 1. There are five participants in this experiment.
> 2. Through the intercom on the wall, you will be given individual discussion time with four other participants. Please note that the conversation is only one-on-one.
> 3. Once all the participants have completed one dialogue with everyone else, the vote will take place. Voting follows the majority rule, and you can vote for yourself as well.
> 4. If you don't vote within the time limit, you'll automatically vote for yourself, so please be careful.
> 5. If there is a tie in the vote, the candidates who are tied will be re-voted. If there is also a tie in the re-voting, one of the candidates for the re-voting will be randomly selected.
> 6. The person who is elected is given the drug through the mechanical device of the right hand.
> 7. When a person receives three doses of that drug, the person dies.
> 8. The experiment will end after repeating the above process a certain number of times. I can't tell you the exact number of times, but I can guarantee that not everyone will die.
>
> I hope everything goes well for you.
